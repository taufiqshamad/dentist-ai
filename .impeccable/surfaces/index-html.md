# Surface Brief: Dentha Landing Page

- **Scope**: Entire landing page (`index.html`) with embedded hero conversational AI triage & booking engine, plus expandable full-modal chat system.
- **Mode**: Persuade (converting visitors into booked patients through immediate direct dialogue).

## Direction contract

**THESIS**: Traditional dental clinic websites hide booking behind tedious static forms or phone numbers; Dentha turns the entire hero into an immediate conversational triage console where patients get instant clarity and book in seconds.

**OWN-WORLD**: Warm travertine and serene oat ground (#FBF9F5, #F4EFE6), deep architectural teal (#0D4848, #134E4A), soft warm slate neutrals (#334155, #64748B), refined editorial typography with high-contrast serifs (Playfair/Newsreader style) paired with clean geometric sans (Plus Jakarta / Inter), and bespoke conversational pill affordances with soft frosted borders.

**STORY**: The visitor lands, instantly feels calm and welcomed by Dr. Sarah's conversational prompt in the hero, clicks a quick triage pill or types their symptom/need, walks through seamless interactive slot selection, and completes a confirmed appointment without leaving the flow.

**FIRST VIEWPORT**: Header with Dentha wordmark and minimal navigation. Hero title "Elevate Your Smile. Start a Conversation." with serene boutique studio ambiance. Centered elevated chat console card featuring Dr. Sarah's active status, welcome bubble, input field with quick-reply pills, and instant interaction.

**FORM**: Grounded boutique architectural clinical sanctuary with live conversational triage console; seed key 166ec82e.
