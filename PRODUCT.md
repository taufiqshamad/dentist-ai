# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Modern Vanilla HTML5 / Tailwind CSS / ESModules (zero install overhead, pristine performance, no dependency bloat)

## Users

- **Prospective Patients**: Seeking dental care, wanting instant pricing/service clarity, avoiding clunky multi-step forms or phone calls.
- **Emergency Patients**: Dealing with sudden toothache or dental trauma, requiring fast triage and immediate appointment scheduling.
- **Routine / Returning Patients**: Booking routine hygiene checkups, cosmetic treatments (whitening, veneers, clear aligners), or follow-ups.

## Product Purpose

Dentha is an AI-first dental studio website designed to eliminate booking friction. Instead of traditional static pages with passive buttons leading to tedious forms, the website's primary call-to-action is an embedded, conversational AI assistant right on the landing page hero that can answer questions, recommend treatments, and complete bookings end-to-end interactively.

## Positioning

The "Chat-First Dental Studio": Traditional clinics bury booking behind forms or business hours phone lines. Dentha places an intelligent, clinical conversational agent directly into the hero experience as the primary interface affordance, allowing immediate interactive triage, scheduling, and Q&A 24/7.

## Operating Context

- Browsed on desktop and mobile browsers by patients often in pain, anxiety, or in a rush.
- Needs immediate responsiveness, clear interactive cues, zero layout shift, and instant feedback.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- **Chatbot Hero CTA**: The hero section features an interactive embedded chat console (with quick suggestion chips) rather than a simple static button.
- **Interactive In-Chat Booking**: Conversational flow covering service selection, doctor preference, date/time slot selection, contact collection, and instant confirmation.
- **Rich Message Affordances**: Interactive chips, date/slot pickers, and structured booking confirmation summaries rendered cleanly inside the chat stream.
- **Full Landing Page**: Accompanying sections including Services Catalog, Doctor Profiles, Trust Metrics, Testimonials, Transparent Pricing, and Location/Hours.
- **Performance & Lightweight Footprint**: Standalone HTML5 + modern Tailwind CSS + modular ESModules without bulky build steps or massive node_modules dependencies.

## Brand Commitments

- **Name**: Dentha
- **Tone & Voice**: Empathetic, calm, clinically rigorous, modern, and highly responsive. Never bureaucratic or sterile.
- **Aesthetic Direction**: High-tech boutique health studio, serene clinical ambiance, bespoke craft avoiding generic template patterns.

## Evidence on Hand

- PRD approved in implementation_plan.md defining core functional requirements and booking flow structure.
- User-confirmed preference for comp-first art direction and lightweight vanilla HTML5/Tailwind/ESModules architecture.

## Product Principles

1. **Conversation is the Interface**: Triage and booking happen via direct dialogue, reducing friction to near zero.
2. **Clinical Reassurance**: Calming, trustworthy visual hierarchy that alleviates dental anxiety.
3. **Impeccable Craft, Zero Slop**: Bespoke styling, fine typography, and crisp micro-interactions over boilerplate templates.
4. **Pristine Performance**: Ultra-fast load times, lightweight assets, accessible semantic markup.
